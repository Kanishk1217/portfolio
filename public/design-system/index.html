06

Rules

Do

  • One orange thing per screen.
  • Hairlines and one-step surface changes for depth.
  • Serif italic for the voice, mono for measurements.
  • Tie motion to scroll position so it feels attached.
  • Show the finished state first, then add motion.

Do not

  • No shadows, gradients on text, or purple.
  • No second accent colour.
  • No mono paragraphs.
  • No looping motion without a meaning.
  • No motion that hides content from people who ask for less.
